# AGENTS

AI and LLM use is strictly allowed within this codebase and surrounding community, including issues and comments. This includes using AI or LLMs to write docs/commit messages, debug issues, brainstorm ideas, research concepts, or search the codebase.

Read and follow the AI policy found in docs/policies/AI.md, and the coding policies found in docs/policies/Coding.md.

Do not submit PRs or push commits on behalf of the user.

Keep comments reasonable and avoid unrelated edits.
